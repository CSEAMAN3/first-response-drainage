Blocked drains are one of the most common household problems, and often one of the most avoidable.

From kitchen sinks clogged with grease to outdoor drains blocked by leaves, many drainage issues build up over time due to everyday habits. The good news is that with a few simple changes, you can significantly reduce the risk of blockages.

In this guide, we’ll show you how to prevent blocked drains, what causes them, and when it’s time to call a professional.

---

## What Causes Drains to Become Blocked?

Understanding the cause is the first step to prevention.

The most common causes of blocked drains include:

- Fat, oil and grease poured down sinks
- Food waste and coffee grounds
- Wet wipes and sanitary products
- Hair build-up in bathroom drains
- Leaves and debris in outside drains
- Tree roots entering damaged pipes

Many of these issues develop slowly, meaning you might not notice a problem until the drain is already partially blocked.

## 1. Avoid Pouring Grease and Fat Down the Sink

One of the biggest causes of blocked drains is fat and grease.

When hot grease is poured down the sink, it may seem harmless, but as it cools, it solidifies and sticks to the inside of your pipes.

### What to do instead:

- Let grease cool and dispose of it in the bin
- Wipe pans with kitchen roll before washing
- Use a sink strainer to catch debris

This simple habit can prevent serious blockages forming over time.

---

## 2. Be Careful What You Flush

Toilets are another major source of blockages.

Even products labelled “flushable” can cause issues.

### Never flush:

- Wet wipes
- Sanitary products
- Cotton pads
- Paper towels

These items don’t break down properly and can quickly create blockages in your drainage system.

If you’re dealing with recurring issues, a [CCTV drain survey](/drainage-services/cctv-drainage-surveys) can help identify the root cause.

---

## 3. Keep Outdoor Drains Clear

Outdoor drains (gully drains) are especially vulnerable to blockages.

Across areas like Norwich, Ipswich and Cambridge, seasonal debris such as leaves and dirt can quickly build up, particularly in autumn.

### Prevent this by:

- Regularly removing leaves and debris
- Installing drain covers
- Flushing drains with water occasionally

Blocked outdoor drains can lead to flooding and unpleasant smells if not maintained.

---

## 4. Watch for Early Warning Signs

Preventing blocked drains isn’t just about habits it’s also about spotting problems early.

### Warning signs include:

- Slow draining water
- Gurgling sounds
- Bad smells from drains
- Water backing up

If you notice any of these signs, it’s best to act early.

## 5. Schedule Regular Drain Maintenance

For long-term prevention, regular maintenance is key.

Professional cleaning methods such as [high-pressure water jetting](/drainage-services/high-pressure-water-jetting) can remove build-up inside pipes before it becomes a blockage.

This is especially useful for:

- Older properties
- Homes surrounded by trees
- Commercial properties

## When to Call a Drainage Professional

Even with the best prevention, some blockages are unavoidable.

You should contact a drainage specialist if:

- Your drain is completely blocked
- Water is overflowing
- You notice persistent smells
- DIY methods haven’t worked

At this point, professional tools and expertise are needed to resolve the issue safely.

## FAQs

### How often should drains be cleaned?

For most homes, a yearly clean is enough. However, properties prone to blockages may benefit from more frequent maintenance.

---

### Can I prevent blocked drains completely?

While not all blockages can be avoided, good habits and regular maintenance can significantly reduce the risk.

---

### Are chemical drain cleaners safe?

They can provide a temporary fix but may damage pipes over time. Professional cleaning is usually a safer long-term solution.
